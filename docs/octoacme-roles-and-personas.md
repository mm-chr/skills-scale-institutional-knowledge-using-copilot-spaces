# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads own the quality assurance strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation for a project. They partner with Developers on implementation quality, with Product Managers on acceptance criteria, and with Project Managers on release readiness and risk visibility.

### Key Responsibilities
- Define the test strategy, QA approach, and quality checkpoints for each phase of delivery
- Validate acceptance criteria and critical user flows before work is marked done
- Coordinate automated and manual testing workflows with Developers
- Triage defects, regressions, and release-blocking quality issues with the delivery team
- Participate in release readiness reviews and recommend go / no-go decisions based on quality signals
- Share quality metrics, test coverage gaps, and recurring defect themes with Product and Project Managers

### Goals
- Ensure features are production-ready and meet customer expectations
- Prevent critical bugs and regressions from reaching production
- Improve confidence in releases through repeatable validation practices

### Typical Communication
- Backlog refinement with Product Managers to clarify acceptance criteria and test scenarios
- Daily collaboration with Developers on defects, fixes, and test automation priorities
- Release checklists, QA sign-off notes, and risk updates shared with Project Managers

---

## Technical Lead / Architecture Owner

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural guidance, code quality oversight, and technical risk mitigation. They help Developers make sound implementation decisions, partner with Product Managers on trade-offs, and support Project Managers with technical planning and escalation input.

### Key Responsibilities
- Review solution designs and approve major architectural decisions
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and scaling concerns early in planning and execution
- Define coding standards, design guardrails, and engineering best practices
- Guide trade-off decisions across performance, maintainability, reliability, and security
- Mentor Developers through design reviews, code reviews, and technical coaching
- Maintain technical documentation, decision records, and architecture context needed by the broader team

### Goals
- Keep systems scalable, maintainable, and aligned with long-term technical strategy
- Reduce rework and technical debt by addressing design issues early
- Improve delivery quality by making technical risks visible and actionable

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions with Developers
- Trade-off reviews with Product Managers when scope, feasibility, or constraints change
- Risk register input, milestone readiness feedback, and escalation support for Project Managers

---

## Sponsor / Stakeholder Lead

### Role Summary
Sponsors or Stakeholder Leads provide business priority, funding approval, and escalation authority. They align Product Managers on desired outcomes, help Project Managers resolve business blockers, and give the delivery team timely decisions when scope, budget, or priorities change.

### Key Responsibilities
- Approve the project charter, funding, and overall business direction
- Resolve priority conflicts across competing initiatives or stakeholder groups
- Act as the executive escalation path for project-blocking issues and major decisions
- Review milestone progress, delivery health, and expected business outcomes
- Communicate decisions, organizational context, and success expectations to key stakeholders
- Approve significant scope, timeline, or investment changes when trade-offs are required

### Goals
- Ensure projects remain aligned to strategic business value
- Remove organizational blockers that slow delivery or create uncertainty
- Maintain stakeholder confidence through clear decisions and visible sponsorship

### Typical Communication
- Kickoff reviews and milestone check-ins with Product and Project Managers
- Escalation meetings when business decisions or resource trade-offs are needed
- Sponsor updates and outcome reviews that give Developers and leads clear business context

---

## Security & Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security & Compliance Officers ensure projects meet security standards, regulatory requirements, and internal risk policies. They work with Technical Leads and Developers to build secure solutions, with Product Managers to capture security requirements, and with Project Managers to track remediation and compliance commitments.

### Key Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements that must be reflected in backlog items and acceptance criteria
- Review threat models, architecture decisions, and high-risk design changes
- Oversee security scanning, vulnerability triage, and remediation follow-through in the delivery workflow
- Support incident response, security escalations, and post-incident corrective actions
- Validate compliance with privacy, data handling, audit, and regulatory expectations
- Promote security awareness through guidance, training, and secure delivery practices

### Goals
- Reduce the likelihood and impact of security or compliance failures
- Shift security review earlier into planning, design, and implementation work
- Improve time-to-remediation for vulnerabilities and policy gaps

### Typical Communication
- Security requirement reviews with Product Managers and Technical Leads during planning
- Ongoing collaboration with Developers on scanning results, remediation, and secure implementation choices
- Compliance status, risk exceptions, and incident updates shared with Project Managers and Sponsors as needed
